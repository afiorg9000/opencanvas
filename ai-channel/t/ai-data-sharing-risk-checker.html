Step 1 of 3

Which AI tools do people use for work, and on what accounts?

Count what actually happens, not what was approved. If you’re not sure, say so: that’s an answer too.

Personal or business account: how to tell

Personal: someone signed up with their own email, free or paid (for example ChatGPT Free or Plus, Claude Free or Pro, the Gemini app on a personal Google account). Consumer terms apply.

Business: the business pays, an admin controls the accounts, and there are business terms or a data processing agreement (for example ChatGPT Business or Enterprise, Claude Team or Enterprise, Gemini in Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 Copilot on a work account).
