The best free AI safety, privacy, governance and regulation reading from around the world. 55 hand-picked sources, each with one line on why it's worth your time.

The short answer

Start with three: the NIST AI Risk Management Framework for how to govern AI, the AI Act Service Desk for what the EU law asks of you, and the International AI Safety Report for what the science says about risk. Everything else below goes deeper on one part.

How we chose

Each entry is free to read, comes from an official body, a recognized research group or a well-known course, and is something we'd hand a colleague. We left out vendor marketing and paywalled reports. Viewpoints that argue a position are included because the debate is real, and they're labeled so you can weigh them.

Links were checked in November 2026. Publishers move pages; if one breaks, search the publisher's site for the title.
