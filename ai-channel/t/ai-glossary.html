About this glossary

This glossary explains ideas; it doesn't take a side on when powerful AI arrives or how likely bad outcomes are — experts disagree by decades and by orders of magnitude, and you'll see that disagreement flagged in the entries. Ideas are credited to the specific people and organisations who made them. Current as of August 2026; forecasts and measurements move, so check the linked sources for today's numbers. Not legal, financial, or career advice.
