Which AI skills does your team actually need?

Probably fewer than the course catalogs suggest, and not the same ones for everybody. Add the roles on your team and answer six plain questions. You’ll get a skills plan by role: who needs what, in what order, and what you don’t need yet.

The six skills it plans for
  • Using AI tools well on real tasks. Your team’s actual work, not prompt tricks.
  • Checking the output, and knowing when not to trust AI. Confident wrong answers, and what catches them.
  • Data and privacy judgment. What never goes into an AI tool, and who decides.
  • Writing the team’s AI rule. One page, for one or two people to own.
  • Redesigning a workflow. Once people use the tools, change the work, not just the drafting.
  • Evaluating AI tools and vendors. For whoever has to say yes to a purchase.

Each one comes back marked everyone, some people or one or two people, with a first session you can run without buying anything. Where the truth is “you don’t need this yet,” it says so.

Answers stay in your browser for this session only. Nothing is stored or sent.
