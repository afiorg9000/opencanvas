Which parts of your team’s work could AI change first?

Help your team adapt to AI without guessing what comes next. Add the roles on your team and answer seven plain questions. In about six minutes you’ll see which tasks AI could change first, where your people still add the most value, and the skills to build first.

What the map shows
  • A task map of your team. Every role split into its main tasks, colored by how much AI could take on.
  • What changes first, and what stays with people. Ranked by team time. Usually not whole jobs, but some of their tasks, starting with the repetitive ones.
  • Priority reskilling areas and a short plan. Including what your AI policy should actually say, and when not to trust AI. Short enough to take to your next team meeting.
Why map it now? Today’s AI tools are like very capable assistants — great at some tasks, hopeless at others. Companies are explicitly trying to build the next step: AI that can do most tasks a person can do at a computer, as well as a skilled human. Experts disagree on when that arrives (serious estimates range from a few years to a few decades), but almost nobody serious says “never,” which is why it’s worth mapping your team’s work now.
