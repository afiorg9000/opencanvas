The version of your business that adapted

Three years from now there are two versions of every business in your industry: the one that spent a few hours a quarter testing, writing rules, and spreading skills — and the one that decided AI was someone else's subject. The gap between them won't be talent or effort. It will be that one of them started while it was still optional. Retake this audit in six months. The questions won't change; your answers should.

Benchmark note. By 2025, 58% of US small businesses reported using generative AI — up from 23% two years earlier — while employers worldwide named skills gaps, not technology, as their top barrier.

Sources: US Chamber of Commerce small-business technology surveys; WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025; METR's live task-length data (metr.org/time-horizons). Figures current as of August 2026; adoption numbers vary by definition and should be treated as directional.
