**Eyebrow:** About AI Channel

## Clear AI guidance, with a practical next step.

AI Channel is a free resource from the Leading Learning Partners Association (LLPA). It helps individuals and organizations understand AI, explore practical tools and find relevant expertise when needed.

#### Who it's for

Business owners, executives, HR and L&D professionals, public-sector teams and individuals exploring AI's impact.

#### What you can do here

Use free assessments and guides, explore evidence and uncertainty, and choose whether to seek additional support.

#### Who runs it

LLPA is a network of independent training and advisory companies. Members may offer paid courses, consulting and implementation services. We distinguish free editorial resources from member services.

#### How we approach evidence

We aim to identify sources, date time-sensitive information and distinguish established findings from estimates and unresolved questions. Specific resources should contain their own citations and limitations.

**What is AI Channel?** A free resource for understanding AI, exploring practical tools and finding relevant next steps. It is run by LLPA.

**Who are the members?** Independent training and advisory companies in LLPA's network. Member services may be paid; free site resources do not require purchasing them.

**Is the search box an AI chatbot?** No. It searches the site's available resources.

**Do the tools store my answers?** Data handling depends on the implemented tool. The live privacy notice must accurately explain what is stored, transmitted or retained. \[Verify before launch.\]

**Are you selling a particular AI product?** AI Channel aims to offer vendor-neutral guidance and disclose relevant commercial relationships.

**How do you decide what's true?** We aim to cite sources, identify publication dates and communicate uncertainty, including when experts disagree.

**Who is AI Channel for?** Business owners, executives, HR and L&D professionals, public-sector teams and individuals.

# 13 · Design

## Design strategy

The rules every page follows. If a new section breaks one of these, change the section, not the rule.

### Principles

- **Simple over impressive.** One idea per section, plenty of white space, short text blocks.
- **Consistent everywhere.** Same type, colours, buttons, cards and spacing on every page, so people learn the site once.
- **Honest.** Only true numbers and claims. No invented testimonials, logos or social proof.
- **Human.** Real photos of people working and learning together, never stock-AI imagery (robots, circuits, glowing brains).
- **Readable for everyone.** Text passes accessibility contrast; nothing depends on colour or animation alone.

### Page structure

Every top-level page uses the same building blocks, in this order where they apply:

1. **Header:** logo, navigation, search, and the Get AI Help button (always visible as you scroll).
2. **Hero:** eyebrow, headline, one-line intro, one primary and one secondary button, then a main image.
3. **Proof points:** short, true facts on the hero image (e.g. number of free tools, no sign-up, independent).
4. **Content sections:** pathway cards, numbered step lists, or featured cards. Each section has a small label, a headline and a one-line intro.
5. **Call-to-action banner:** "Need help putting AI to work?" with Get AI Help, before the footer. Left off pages that already end with that ask (pathways, Get AI Help).
6. **Footer:** newsletter sign-up, AI Channel and LLPA logos, link columns, Privacy · Terms · Cookies.

Not used yet: testimonials and social media links. Add them only when there are real quotes and real accounts.

### Typography

- **One font:** Inter. No serif.
- **Three weights:** 400 for body text, 600 for buttons and links, 800 for headings.
- **One size scale (px):** 12 · 14 · 15 · 17 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 56. Body text is 17.
- **Hierarchy:** page headline → section headline → card title → body → small label. Labels are small, uppercase and spaced out.
- **Alignment:** left-aligned everywhere except the Home hero and the call-to-action banner.
- **Line length:** body paragraphs stop at about 68 characters.

### Colour

- **Base:** off-white page, white bands, light grey bands, dark navy text.
- **Accent:** one blue for buttons, links and labels.
- **Text greys:** two only, both passing accessibility contrast.
- **Dark blocks:** blue-to-navy or orange-to-purple gradient with a fine dot grid. Used for featured tools, interactive cards, the call-to-action banner, the Get AI Help row and the footer.

### Shapes and spacing

- **Corners:** three sizes only: pill (buttons, tags), small (12px), card (20px).
- **Spacing scale (px):** 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64. Large gaps between sections, smaller gaps inside them.

### Components

- **Buttons:** primary (blue), secondary (outlined), light (white, on dark blocks). Button text says what happens: "Take the assessment →", not "Click here".
- **Cards:** photo on top, label, title, one-line description, link. Same layout for role cards and featured cards.
- **Step lists:** numbered rows with label, title, description and arrow. Used for pathways and AI & Society.
- **States:** cards lift on hover, buttons press in when clicked, links underline on hover, keyboard focus shows a blue ring, disabled buttons fade.

### Motion and interaction (the 3–5 upgrades we use)

1. Hover effects on every clickable card and button.
2. Smooth entrance animations as sections scroll into view (turned off for people who prefer reduced motion).
3. Layered design: proof-point cards overlapping the hero image.
4. Sticky call to action: Get AI Help stays in the header.
5. Elevated typography: one font, a strict size scale, bold tight headings.

Not used, on purpose: video backgrounds, sticky stacking sections, full-screen menus. They add weight without helping people find a tool faster.

### Imagery

- Real photos of people at work, in workshops and in conversation, from LLPA events where possible.
- Warm, natural light; avoid posed stock looks.
- Each role card and pathway uses a different photo; never repeat a photo on the same page.
